Biography

Boncybee is an electronic music artist whose bold and captivating style blends bass house, Majestic, and pop to deliver intense and immersive sonic experiences. Deeply influenced by pioneers of the scene such as Martin Garrix and Skrillex, Boncybee has, over the past five years, crafted a personal and distinctive universe that now draws the attention of an audience seeking novelty and emotional depth.
